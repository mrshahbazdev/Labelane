Labelane Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 September 2026

Labelane is a label and barcode designer for Windows, published by Muhammad Shahbaz. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it.

Data we collect

None. Labelane does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal data. It has no account system, no analytics, no advertising and no telemetry, and it makes no network requests.

Data stored on your computer

Templates you save, and PDFs you export, are written to the application's own folder in your Windows user profile. Spreadsheets, CSV files and images you open are read only when you choose them, and only to display and print your labels. None of this leaves your computer.

Printing

Labels are sent only to printers installed on your computer, and only when you press Print.

Removing your data

Uninstalling Labelane removes its folder, including saved templates, from %LOCALAPPDATA%\Packages.

Children

Labelane does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
